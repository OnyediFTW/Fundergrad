# The 14 weeks

| Week of | Event | Choices (game costs) | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Sep 7 | Textbook shopping | All new $620 / Used + rental $340 / Borrow + library $150 (stress risk) | Textbook and college costs |
| 2. Sep 14 | Free stuff at the credit card table | Sign up (limit $500, ~22% APR) / Decline / Sign up and pay in full | Credit cards and APR |
| 3. Sep 21 | Friends invite you to a concert | Go $85 (cash or card) / Skip / $20 hangout | Wants vs. needs, spending limits |
| 4. Sep 28 | Free trials become ~$35/month | Cancel all / Keep one / Ignore | Free trials and subscriptions |
| 5. Oct 5 | First paycheck is delayed | Use savings / Cut spending / Use the card / Ask family (overdraft fee $35 below $0) | Overdraft fees and buffers |
| 6. Oct 12 | Fall Break: dining halls close | Go home $90 / Stay (food +$40) / Friend's trip $70 | Planning for known costs |
| 7. Oct 19 | Laptop dies during midterms | Repair $180 / Refurbished $350 / New $700 / Borrow (stress up) | Emergency funds |
| 8. Oct 26 | Boss offers extra shifts (+$80) | Take them (stress) / Decline / Halloween party $35 | Income vs. time |
| 9. Nov 2 | Roommate asks to borrow $90 | Lend $90 / Lend $30 / Decline | Lending to friends |
| 10. Nov 9 | Job offer with a check to deposit | Ignore and report / Deposit and send $250 back | Scams |
| 11. Nov 16 | $240 jacket with "pay in 4" | Buy now (4 x $60) / Wait 48 hours / Skip | Buy now, pay later |
| 12. Nov 23 | Thanksgiving | Bus or fly $140 / Carpool $45 / Friendsgiving $30 | Planning for travel costs |
| 13. Nov 30 | Winter shopping, gifts, dues | New coat (total $190) / Thrifted coat (total $135) | Saving for known expenses |
| 14. Dec 7 | Finals week | Cope spending ~$70 / Cook and plan ~$20 | Finishing strong |
