## Which numbers are sourced and which are game estimates

Sourced: textbook cost range (College Board), card APR of about 22% (Federal Reserve G.19, Q2 2026), overdraft fee of $35 (CFPB).
Game estimates: all other prices and the weekly pay of $190.
